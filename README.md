# Moodlings — Product Case Study

Moodlings is an iOS emotional reflection product designed to help people move beyond broad labels like “good” or “bad” and identify what they are actually feeling.

[View the case study →](https://zerozero0713.github.io/moodlings-case-study/)

## Project Overview

I independently researched, designed, and developed Moodlings as a working iOS product. The project translates insights from my behavioral and counseling background into product requirements, interaction flows, and a functional SwiftUI application supported by Firebase.

## My Role

- Product strategy
- Research synthesis
- Requirements definition
- UX/UI design
- Interaction design
- SwiftUI implementation
- Product iteration

## Product Decisions

Moodlings was designed around a few core decisions:

- Make emotional vocabulary easier to explore without telling users how they should feel.
- Connect individual check-ins into patterns over time.
- Keep reflection lightweight and optional.
- Give users control over what they include in reports brought into counseling.
- Offer support for reflection without automatically sharing personal information.

## Building the Product

The product was designed in Figma and implemented as a working iOS prototype using SwiftUI, Firebase, and Google Sign-In.

Implementation became part of the product discovery process: technical constraints helped clarify priorities, expose incomplete interaction decisions, and shape a more focused experience.
